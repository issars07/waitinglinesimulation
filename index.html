Distribution Types (M/M/S uses Exponential):

For M/M/S, arrivals follow a Poisson distribution, and both inter-arrival and service times are exponentially distributed. Other distributions are for demonstration purposes and deviate from a strict M/M/S.

Adjust parameters and click START.

Theory vs. Simulation

Theory = M/M/S steady-state formulas. Simulated = long-run averages from this run, with a 95% confidence interval (batch means). A measure is consistent when the theoretical value falls inside the interval. Click a row to chart how it converges.
